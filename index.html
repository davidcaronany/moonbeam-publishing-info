Moonbeam YouTube Publishing turns completed Moonbeam bedtime stories into narrated videos for the owner's YouTube channel.

What the application does

The channel owner connects their Google account, selects the Moonbeam channel, and enables public or private uploads. Moonbeam then creates videos from completed story illustrations and narration, including the Moonbeam opening and sleepy owl ending.

Uploads are marked as made for kids and organized into themed playlists. The owner can pause publishing, check upload progress, and disconnect the account.

This is an owner-operated publishing tool for Moonbeam — Bedtime Stories for Kids. Viewers do not connect their Google accounts to this tool.

Why Google access is requested

Google authorization lets Moonbeam upload videos, read the connected channel's identity to confirm the correct destination, and find or create playlists and add the uploaded stories to them.

The requested permissions are YouTube upload access, read access, and the YouTube management permission used by the playlist API. Google's consent wording may describe broader capabilities; the implemented publishing workflow uses these permissions for channel checks, uploads and playlist organization.

Authorization credentials are kept on the computer running this local Moonbeam installation. The application sends videos and their publishing details to YouTube when the owner enables publishing. Public uploads are available to anyone on YouTube.

Read how publishing data is used, stored and removed.

Operator and support

Moonbeam YouTube Publishing is operated by the owner of the Moonbeam channel linked above. Account-connection and publishing questions can be sent to the support email displayed on the application's Google authorization screen.

This information concerns the publishing integration. It does not describe the separate providers used to generate Moonbeam stories, artwork or narration.
